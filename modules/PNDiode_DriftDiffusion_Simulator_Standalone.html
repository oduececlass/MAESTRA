Equation map

Each plot is tied to one part of the coupled device model.

Electrostatics

$$\frac{d^2\psi}{dx^2}=-\frac{q}{\varepsilon}(p-n+N_D^+-N_A^-)$$

Charge density sets potential curvature; $E=-d\psi/dx$.

Carrier conservation

$$\frac{dJ_n}{dx}=qR,\qquad \frac{dJ_p}{dx}=-qR$$

Recombination couples the electron and hole continuity equations.

Drift + diffusion

$$J_n=q\mu_n nE+qD_n\frac{dn}{dx},\qquad J_p=q\mu_p pE-qD_p\frac{dp}{dx}$$

The Scharfetter–Gummel discretization preserves stable current evaluation across steep junction gradients.

From rubber sheet to drift–diffusion

The rubber-sheet metaphor represents electrostatic potential. Fixed depletion charge bends the sheet according to Poisson’s equation. The sheet’s slope is related to electric field.

$$\rho(x)\longrightarrow \frac{d^2\psi}{dx^2},\qquad E(x)=-\frac{d\psi}{dx}$$

A PN diode requires one additional layer: electrons and holes move on this landscape. Their drift and diffusion currents, together with recombination and continuity, must be solved self-consistently with Poisson’s equation.

$$\{\psi,n,p\}\ \text{are coupled and solved iteratively.}$$
