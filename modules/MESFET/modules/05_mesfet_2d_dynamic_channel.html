Channel-control model

Local depletion width
\(W(x)=a\sqrt{\dfrac{V_{bi}-V_{GS}+V(x)}{V_P}}\)
Conducting-channel opening
\(h(x)=\max\!\left[0,\,a-W(x)\right]\)
Onset of drain-side pinch-off
\(V_{DS,sat}=V_P-V_{bi}+V_{GS}=V_{GS}-V_T\)

The visualization uses the gradual-channel approximation. In saturation, the channel is shown pinched near the drain-side gate edge and the excess drain voltage appears across a short high-field region.
