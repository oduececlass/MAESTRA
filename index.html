Suggested Learning Path

1. FoundationsProbability, Fermi-Dirac function, DOS, carrier concentration, Fermi level.
2. ElectrostaticsPoisson equation, depletion approximation, electric field, potential, band diagrams.
3. PN DiodeEquilibrium, bias, minority carrier injection, drift-diffusion, diode equation.
4. Bipolar TransistorsBJT structure, transistor action, current gain, operating modes, switching, nonideal effects, and HBT extensions.
5. Field-Effect + Energy DevicesMOSFET and MESFET channel control, PNPN/thyristor switching, solar cells, generation/recombination, and efficiency.
